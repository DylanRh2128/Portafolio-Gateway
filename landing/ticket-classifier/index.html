Support Ticket Classifier Bot

An n8n workflow watches a Gmail inbox and posts every new email to this API's webhook. The API classifies it with an LLM — forced to answer through a fixed schema, then re-validated server-side — and stores the ticket for history and stats. Here you play the customer.
